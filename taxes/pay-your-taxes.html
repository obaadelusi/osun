    Osun Internal Revenue Service (OsunIRS)

    Pay Your Taxes

    Pay personal income tax, PAYE, levies and other State revenue securely online, at a bank, or at an OsunIRS tax station — and receive an automated revenue receipt.

    Pay only through official channels

    OsunIRS does not work through agents or third parties. Pay only on the official e-payment portal, at participating banks, to accredited revenue marshals or at an OsunIRS tax station — and always ask for your Automated Revenue Receipt.

    How to pay in three steps

    Every step is completed on the official Osun State e-payment portal managed by OsunIRS.

    1. 1

      Get your Payer ID

      Your Payer ID identifies you or your business on the State revenue system. If you have paid before, it is printed at the top-left of your previous revenue receipt.

      • Register for a Payer ID
      • Retrieve an existing Payer ID
    2. 2

      Generate a payment invoice (IPN)

      Enter your Payer ID, choose your tax office and the revenue item you are paying — for example PAYE, revenue code 401001 — and add a narration such as “PAYE for October 2026”.

      • Generate an IPN
    3. 3

      Pay and keep your receipt

      Pay the IPN online by card or bank transfer, or take it to a participating bank or POS. Your Automated Revenue Receipt is issued once payment is confirmed.

      • Pay with your invoice
      • Verify a receipt

    What are you paying?

    Choose the tax or levy that applies to you to see who pays it, when it is due and where to find full guidance.

    • PAYE (Pay-As-You-Earn)

      Income tax deducted from employees’ salaries each month and remitted to OsunIRS by the employer.

      Who pays
      Employers, on behalf of their staff
      When due
      By the 10th of the following month
      PAYE guidance

    • Direct Assessment

      Self-assessed income tax: work out your tax, pay at a designated bank or online, and receive an e-TCC without visiting a tax office.

      Who pays
      Self-employed people, traders and professionals
      When due
      Annual return by 31 March
      Direct Assessment guidance

    • Harmonised Bill (business levies)

      Pay all ministry, department and agency annual levies in one bill. Certificates and receipts are sent to your registered email address.

      Who pays
      Businesses and organisations
      When due
      Yearly, as stated on your bill
      Request your harmonised bill

    • Land Use Charge

      A single charge that consolidates property tax, tenement rate and ground rent payable in the State.

      Who pays
      Property owners and occupiers
      When due
      As stated on your demand notice
      Land Use Charge guidance

    • Stamp Duty

      A tax on written documents and certain transactions between individuals, such as tenancy agreements and sale deeds.

      Who pays
      Individuals who sign qualifying documents
      When due
      When the document is signed
      Stamp Duty guidance

    • Motor Vehicle Licensing

      Vehicle licence, registration and related fees for vehicles in Osun State.

      Who pays
      Vehicle owners
      When due
      On registration and at each renewal
      View licensing fees

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a Payer ID and where do I find mine?

    A Payer ID is your unique number on the Osun State revenue system. It is printed at the top-left of any automated revenue receipt you have received before. If you have never paid, register for one on the e-payment portal; if you have lost it, use “Retrieve your Payer ID”.

    What is an IPN?

    An IPN (Invoice Payment Number) is a payment invoice generated for a specific revenue item and amount. Banks and the online portal use it to credit your payment to the right account, so always pay against an IPN.

    My employer deducts PAYE. Do I still need to file a return?

    Your employer remits and files returns for the PAYE it deducts. If you also earn other income — such as rent, a business or freelance work — you must file your own annual return by 31 March and pay any extra tax through Direct Assessment.

    Will I pay any income tax on a low income?

    From 1 January 2026, the first ₦800,000 of annual chargeable income is taxed at 0%, so many low-income earners pay no personal income tax. Use the estimator above to check your position.

    I paid but did not receive a receipt. What should I do?

    Keep your bank teller or transfer evidence and contact OsunIRS customer care with your IPN and payment reference. Once the payment is confirmed you can verify and download your receipt online.

    How do I get a Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC)?

    When your tax for the preceding three years has been assessed and paid, apply to OsunIRS for an electronic TCC. Anyone you give it to can check that it is genuine on the OsunIRS verification portal.
