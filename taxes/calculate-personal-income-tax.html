    How personal income tax works from 2026

    The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 introduced major reforms for personal income tax across Nigeria and Osun State, effective 1 January 2026.

    • 0% tax-free threshold

      The first ₦800,000 of your annual chargeable income is taxed at 0%. If your chargeable income is ₦800,000 or less (about ₦66,667 a month), you pay no personal income tax.

      Exempt amount
      First ₦800,000 a year
      Rate
      0%

    • 20% rent relief

      Tenants can deduct 20% of the rent they pay on their home each year, up to a maximum deduction of ₦500,000.

      Relief
      20% of annual rent
      Maximum
      ₦500,000 a year

    • Progressive bands

      A higher rate applies only to the part of your income inside that band, not to all of it. For example, with ₦2,000,000 of chargeable income you pay 15% on ₦1,200,000, after the free ₦800,000.

      Bands
      6 bands
      Rates
      0%, 15%, 18%, 21%, 23%, 25%

    Worked examples

    See how the 2026 rules work out at different income levels.

    Example 1: earning ₦750,000 a year

    With no deductions, chargeable income is ₦750,000. All of it falls in the first band (0%), so the tax is ₦0 and the effective rate is 0.0%.

    Example 2: earning ₦4,000,000 a year, with pension and rent

    Pension of ₦320,000 (8%) and rent of ₦1,000,000 (relief ₦200,000) bring deductions to ₦520,000 and chargeable income to ₦3,480,000.

    • First ₦800,000 at 0%: ₦0
    • Next ₦2,200,000 at 15%: ₦330,000
    • Remaining ₦480,000 at 18%: ₦86,400

    Total tax: ₦416,400 a year (about ₦34,700 a month), an effective rate of 10.4%.

    Example 3: earning ₦15,000,000 a year

    Pension and life assurance of ₦1,200,000, plus capped rent relief of ₦500,000, bring deductions to ₦1,700,000 and chargeable income to ₦13,300,000.

    • First ₦800,000 at 0%: ₦0
    • Next ₦2,200,000 at 15%: ₦330,000
    • Next ₦9,000,000 at 18%: ₦1,620,000
    • Remaining ₦1,300,000 at 21%: ₦273,000

    Total tax: ₦2,223,000 a year (about ₦185,250 a month), an effective rate of 14.8%.

    Approved Deductions & Reliefs

    Pension contribution

    Contributions to an approved pension scheme under the Pension Reform Act. For employees this is usually 8% of basic, housing and transport pay.

    Rent relief

    20% of the rent you pay on the home you live in, up to ₦500,000 for the tax year.

    Housing and health insurance

    Contributions to the National Housing Fund (NHF) and the National Health Insurance Scheme (NHIS).

    Life assurance premiums

    Premiums paid on your own or your spouse’s life to an insurer licensed in Nigeria.

    Tax Calculation Frequently Asked Questions

    Is this estimate binding?

    No. It is an estimate based on the rates in the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. Your final liability is set when OsunIRS assesses your return.

    My employer deducts PAYE. Do I still need this?

    Yes. You can check that your payroll deductions match the 2026 rates and that your pension and rent relief are being applied, and see what you may owe on other income.

    I am self-employed. Which tax applies to me?

    Sole proprietors, professionals and other self-employed people pay personal income tax by Direct Assessment at the same rates, and file an annual return by 31 March.

    How is rent relief checked?

    Keep your tenancy agreement and proof of rent payments. OsunIRS may ask for them when it reviews your return or issues a Tax Clearance Certificate.

    What is the difference between gross and chargeable income?

    Gross income is everything you earn before deductions. Chargeable income is what remains after pension, rent relief and other approved deductions, and the tax bands apply only to that amount.
