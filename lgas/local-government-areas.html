    Three senatorial districts

    The 30 Local Government Areas of Osun State, grouped by senatorial district.

    Osun Central

    Includes the state capital, Osogbo, and the towns around it.

      Osun East (Ife / Ijesa)

      Covers Ile-Ife, Ilesa and the Ife and Ijesa communities.

        Osun West

        Includes Iwo, Ede, Ejigbo and the western communities of the state.

          What your local government does

          Local governments are the tier of government closest to you. Their secretariats are where many everyday services start.

          Birth registration

          Every Local Government Secretariat hosts a National Population Commission desk for registering births.

          Find your registration desk

          Primary healthcare

          Local governments run ward Primary Health Centres for immunisation, antenatal care and basic treatment.

          Proof of local origin

          Your local government issues the letter confirming your town and LGA of origin, which is needed for a State of Origin certificate.

          Frequently asked questions

          How many Local Government Areas does Osun State have?

          Osun State has 30 Local Government Areas. The state also runs one Area Office, the Ife East Area Office in Modakeke.

          Is my LGA the same as my senatorial district?

          No. A senatorial district is a larger grouping of several LGAs. Osun Central, Osun East and Osun West each contain about ten.
