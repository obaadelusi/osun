    Recruitment is free: beware of job scams

    The Osun State Government never charges a fee for applications, interviews, medicals or appointment letters. Do not pay any person or send money to a personal account to secure a job. Report anyone who asks for payment to the Civil Service Commission.

    How to apply

    Every Osun State Government vacancy follows the same open and merit-based process.

    1. 1

      Check the requirements

      Read the vacancy summary, qualifications and closing date. Choose a vacancy that matches your qualifications and note its reference number.

      • Browse vacancies
    2. 2

      Prepare your documents

      Gather a current CV, your credentials and certificates, your NIN slip, your State of Origin certificate (from your Local Government) and a passport photograph. Professional roles also need a valid practising licence.

      • Birth registration and age attestation
    3. 3

      Submit and prepare for screening

      Submit your application to the Civil Service Commission before the closing date, quoting the reference number. Shortlisted candidates are contacted by email and phone for document screening and interview. Offers are published on this portal.

      • Contact the Commission

    • Pension and job security

      Permanent staff are enrolled in the Contributory Pension Scheme and covered by the Osun Health Insurance Scheme.

      Staff receive in-service training and are promoted on merit under the Civil Service Rules.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do I have to pay to apply for a government job?

    No. Application, screening, interview and appointment are free. Anyone asking for money is committing fraud; report them to the Civil Service Commission or the police.

    Do I need to be an indigene of Osun State?

    Most vacancies are open to all Nigerians who meet the qualifications. Where a vacancy is restricted to indigenes, the summary says so and you must provide a State of Origin certificate from your Local Government.

    What is the maximum age for applicants?

    Entry-level posts normally require applicants to be 35 or younger. Professional and senior posts state their own age limit in the vacancy. You may be asked for an age declaration or birth certificate.

    When will I hear back after applying?

    Shortlisted candidates are contacted within four weeks of the closing date. If you have not been contacted by then, your application was not shortlisted.

    Questions about a vacancy?
