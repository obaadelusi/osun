    Official reference copies and Certified True Copies (CTC)

    Electronic summaries and reference texts on this portal are provided free of charge for public information, civic awareness, and legal research. Pursuant to the Evidence Act and the Interpretation Law of Osun State, any person tendering a State Law, Subsidiary Instrument, or Official Gazette before a court of law or tribunal must obtain an authenticated Certified True Copy (CTC) from the Osun State Ministry of Justice or the Government Printer in Osogbo.

    How a Bill becomes Law and is published in the Official Gazette

    Under the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria 1999 (as amended) and the Standing Orders of the Osun State House of Assembly, every State Law follows a transparent three-stage constitutional process.

    1. 1

      Legislative Passage (House of Assembly)

      An Executive Bill or Member's Bill undergoes First and Second Readings in the Osun State House of Assembly, public hearings before the relevant Standing Committee where citizens submit memoranda, clause-by-clause consideration in the Committee of the Whole, and Third Reading passage.

      • View current House Bills
    2. 2

      Governor's Assent & Enrolment

      The Clerk of the House transmits the clean copy of the passed Bill, vetted by the Directorate of Legislative Drafting in the Ministry of Justice, to the Executive Governor for formal Assent. Once signed, Sealed copies are enrolled at the High Court of Justice Registry.

      • Browse enacted State Laws
    3. 3

      Official Gazetting & Public Access

      The Government Printer at the Osun State Government Press publishes the enacted Law, Executive Order, or Legal Notice in the Osun State of Nigeria Official Gazette with a Volume and Gazette Number, making it available for public purchase and Certified True Copy issuance.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are online copies of Osun State Laws admissible in court?

    Online summaries and digital reference copies are provided for public information and legal research. Under the Evidence Act, if you need to tender a State Law, Subsidiary Instrument, or Official Gazette in court or administrative proceedings, you should obtain a stamped Certified True Copy (CTC) from the Osun State Ministry of Justice or the Government Printer, or tender an official printed volume published by authority of the State Government.

    How do I publish a Change of Name or statutory notice in the Osun State Official Gazette?

    First, swear to an Affidavit of Change of Name or Declaration before a High Court or Magistrate Court Registry. Next, generate an OsunIRS Invoice Payment Number (IPN) for Official Gazette publication, pay the statutory fee (₦7,500 for personal change of name), and submit your court affidavit, NIN slip, passport photograph, and payment receipt to the Government Printer at the Osun State Government Press in Osogbo.

    How do I cite a Law of Osun State in legal documents?

    Consolidated laws in the Revised Edition are cited by their short title and Chapter number — for example, “Osun State Revenue Administration Law, Cap. O15, Laws of Osun State of Nigeria”. Laws enacted after the last consolidated revision are cited by their short title, year of enactment, and Official Gazette Volume and Number.

    Where can law firms, libraries, and researchers buy the bound Volumes of the Laws of Osun State?

    Official printed sets of the Revised Edition of the Laws of Osun State and annual supplements are available for purchase through the Directorate of Law Review and Legislative Drafting at the Ministry of Justice, State Secretariat, Abere, Osogbo, upon presentation of a verified OsunIRS treasury receipt.

    How can residents or civil society groups contribute to Bills before the Osun State House of Assembly?

    After a Bill passes Second Reading in the Osun State House of Assembly, the relevant Standing Committee schedules a public hearing and invites written memoranda from residents, professional bodies, traditional institutions, and civil society organisations. Memoranda can be submitted to the Office of the Clerk at the Assembly Complex, Abere, Osogbo.

    Where can I verify Chieftaincy Declarations or Local Government Bye-Laws?

    Registered Chieftaincy Declarations made under the Chiefs Law are gazetted and custodied jointly by the Ministry of Local Government and Chieftaincy Affairs, the Ministry of Justice, and the Government Press. Local Government Bye-Laws can be inspected at the respective Local Government Council Secretariat or the Government Press archive in Osogbo.

    Need help with State Laws or Official Gazettes?

    The Osun State Ministry of Justice, House of Assembly Clerk's Office, and Government Press are open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM.
