    Register within 60 days — child birth registration is free

    Under the Births, Deaths, etc. (Compulsory Registration) Act, every child born in Osun State must be registered within 60 days of birth. Registration of newborns and children at National Population Commission (NPC) desks and public health facilities is free of charge. Do not pay unaccredited agents, and ensure you receive a verifiable NPC Certificate of Birth.

    How to register a birth in three steps

    Birth registration in Osun State is digitised on the National Population Commission electronic Civil Registration and Vital Statistics (e-CRVS) platform and linked with the National Identification Number (NIN).

    1. 1

      Get a Birth Notification and Parent's NIN

      Collect the medical Birth Notification slip from the hospital, Primary Health Centre, or maternity clinic where the baby was delivered. At least one parent or legal informant must have a valid 11-digit National Identification Number (NIN).

      • Find a NIN enrolment centre (NIMC)
      • Check documents for home deliveries
    2. 2

      Pre-register online or visit an NPC centre

      Begin your application on the official NPC e-CRVS (VitalReg) self-service portal, or present your Birth Notification slip and NIN to an accredited NPC Registrar at your hospital, Primary Health Centre, or Local Government Secretariat.

      • Start on NPC e-CRVS portal
      • Find an Osun LGA registration desk
    3. 3

      Collect and verify your Birth Certificate

      Once the Registrar verifies and approves the record, the birth is entered into the National Civil Register and an official digital and printable Certificate of Birth with a QR verification code is issued.

      • Download or verify a certificate

    Which registration service do you need?

    Select the category that matches the age of the person being registered to see who can apply, statutory fees, and required supporting documents.

    • Newborn Registration (0 – 60 days)

      Standard compulsory registration for babies within 60 days of delivery at a hospital, Primary Health Centre, maternity clinic, or home in Osun State.

      Who applies
      Mother, father or authorised informant
      Statutory fee
      Free (₦0)
      View newborn requirements

    • Child & Minor Registration (61 days – 17 years)

      Late registration for infants, school-age children, and minors under 18 years whose birth was not registered within the initial 60-day window.

      Who applies
      Parents or legal guardian
      Statutory fee
      Free at NPC & PHC centres (court affidavit if required)
      View child & minor requirements

    • Adult Birth Attestation (18 years & above)

      Official Certificate of Birth Attestation for adults aged 18 and older whose birth was not registered at childhood. Required for Nigerian passports, visas, employment, and civil service verification.

      Who applies
      Adults aged 18 years and older
      Statutory fee
      Official NPC portal fee via Remita
      View Birth Attestation requirements

    • Certified True Copy & Verification

      Digitise an older paper NPC birth certificate, verify certificate authenticity, or apply for a Certified True Copy if your original certificate was lost or damaged.

      Who applies
      Certificate holder or parent of minor
      Statutory fee
      Official e-CRVS verification fee
      Verify or replace on e-CRVS

    Frequently asked questions

    Who is legally authorised to issue a Birth Certificate in Osun State?

    The National Population Commission (NPC) is the sole statutory body mandated by Federal law to issue official Birth Certificates and Certificates of Birth Attestation in Osun State and across Nigeria. Hospital birth notifications and local government declarations are supporting records, not substitutes for an NPC Birth Certificate.

    Is a hospital Birth Notification slip the same as a Birth Certificate?

    No. A hospital, Primary Health Centre, or maternity clinic issues a medical Birth Notification confirming the date, time, and parentage at delivery. You must present that notification to an NPC Registrar (at the health facility, an LGA office, or via the e-CRVS portal) to be issued the official Certificate of Birth.

    Do both parents need a National Identification Number (NIN) to register a child?

    At least one parent or legal informant must provide a valid 11-digit National Identification Number (NIN) to link the child's birth record on the electronic Civil Registration and Vital Statistics (e-CRVS) system. Providing both parents' NINs is strongly recommended.

    What if my baby was born at home or outside a hospital?

    You can still register your baby free of charge. Visit your nearest Primary Health Centre (PHC) or the NPC Vital Registration Desk at your Local Government Secretariat with your NIN, the child's immunisation card, and a signed confirmation from the traditional birth attendant, community head (Baálẹ̀ / Ọba), or religious leader.

    I am 18 years or older and do not have a Birth Certificate. What should I do?

    Adults aged 18 years and above cannot apply for a child birth certificate. Instead, apply for an official Certificate of Birth Attestation on the NPC e-CRVS self-service portal using your NIN and a sworn Affidavit of Age Declaration obtained from a High Court or Magistrate Court.

    How do I correct a spelling mistake on a Birth Certificate?

    Visit the National Population Commission Osun State Directorate in Osogbo or the LGA office where the birth was registered. Bring the original certificate, the hospital Birth Notification slip, and your NIN slip so an authorised Vital Registration Officer can process a formal amendment on the e-CRVS registry.

    Need help with birth registration?

    The National Population Commission (NPC) Osun State Directorate and LGA Vital Registration desks are open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM.
