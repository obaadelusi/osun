    UNESCO World Heritage Site

    Osun-Osogbo Sacred Grove

    Osogbo LGA, Osogbo

    Ancient spiritual sanctuary along the winding banks of the sacred Osun river.

    The Osun-Osogbo Sacred Grove is one of the last remaining remnants of primary high forest in southern Nigeria. Designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2005, the sacred landscape is dedicated to Osun, the Yoruba goddess of fertility and waters.

    Opening Hours

    Monday to Sunday: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Annual festival peak takes place every August)

    Admission & Fees

    Adults: ₦500 | Students & Children: ₦200 | International Visitors: ₦2,000 | Camera Permit: ₦1,000

    Tour Guides

    Official NCMM accredited tour guides stationed at the entrance pavilion. Available in English, Yorùbá, and French.

    Accessibility & Terrain

    Shaded unpaved natural forest trails. Gentle to moderate walking difficulty. Comfortable flat shoes recommended.

    Rules & Etiquette

    Strict environmental preservation: no littering, swimming, or fishing in the sacred river. Shoes must be removed when entering designated sanctums.

    Facilities

    Visitor reception centre, traditional arts and crafts market, restrooms, parking area at entrance.

    Directions & Transit

    10 minutes drive south of Osogbo city centre along Heritage Site Road. Direct signposts from Olaiya Junction.
