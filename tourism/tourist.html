    Tourist Centres

    Discover the rich cultural heritage, history, and natural beauty of Osun State.

    Osun-Osogbo Sacred Grove

    A UNESCO World Heritage Site located along the banks of the Osun river, housing shrines and sanctuaries dedicated to the fertility goddess.

    Plan your visit

    Olumirin Waterfall

    Located in Erin-Ijesha, this breathtaking natural wonder features seven distinct cascading levels surrounded by lush mountains.

    Plan your visit

    Oke Maria

    A Catholic Marian shrine nestled in Otan Ayegbaju, known for its annual pilgrimage and panoramic views of the surrounding valleys.

    Plan your visit

    Oduduwa Shrine and Grove

    Situated in Ile-Ife, the cradle of the Yoruba race. It marks the spot where Oduduwa, the legendary progenitor of the Yoruba people, is revered.

    Plan your visit

    Moremi Statue of Liberty

    Standing tall in Ile-Ife, this monumental statue celebrates the legacy of Moremi Ajasoro, a courageous heroine in Yoruba folklore.

    Plan your visit

    Osogbo Civic Centre

    A hub of cultural and civic activities, showcasing local arts, history, and the vibrant contemporary life of the state capital.

    Plan your visit
